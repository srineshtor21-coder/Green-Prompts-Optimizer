AI Energy Facts

Training vs. Usage

While training GPT-3 consumed ~1,287 MWh (equal to 120 US homes for a year), the cumulative energy from billions of daily queries is now far greater.

Per-Query Impact

A single ChatGPT query uses approximately 2.9 Wh, about 10x more than a Google search (0.3 Wh). With millions of queries daily, optimization makes a real difference.

Global Scale

Data centers that power AI already consume 1-2% of global electricity. This is projected to reach 3-8% by 2030 as AI adoption grows.

Carbon Intensity

AI's carbon footprint depends on where it runs. The same model query generates 30% more CO2 in coal-powered regions vs. renewable-powered data centers.
